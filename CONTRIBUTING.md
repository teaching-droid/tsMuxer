# Contributing

## Issue follow-up and closure

To keep the tracker manageable, issues marked *awaiting feedback* normally stay open for 14 days. A friendly reminder may be posted after 7 days, and if there is no reply after 14 days, the issue may be closed.

More time is allowed when testing needs special hardware, sample files, or other circumstances.

Issues with a fix confirmed by the reporter or another user may be closed once the fix is released.

A closed issue is never final. Open still means unfinished, and an issue closed after waiting for a reply is paused, not forgotten. If you have new information about the same problem, comment on it or open a new issue that links to it.

One issue is for one problem. A different problem, even a related one, goes into an issue of its own, so that each can be followed and closed on its own. Comments that are not about the problem of an issue may be moved to a new issue or hidden.

When an issue is closed, kept open longer, or split up is decided by the maintainer, case by case.
