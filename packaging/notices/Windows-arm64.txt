libass and the libraries it uses  (draw ASS and SSA subtitles)
================================================================================

tsMuxeR.exe draws ASS and SSA subtitles with libass. libass and the libraries it
needs are shipped as separate dynamic-link libraries placed next to tsMuxeR.exe.
tsMuxeR.exe is not linked against them: it loads ass-9.dll when an ASS or SSA
track is drawn, and works without it (the subtitles are then drawn as plain
text). You may replace any of them with your own build of a compatible version.

How they are built:
    The libraries are built by vcpkg (https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg) from
    packaging/libass/vcpkg.json in this repository, whose builtin-baseline
    fixes every version above, for ARM64 (arm64-windows), with the build
    configuration recorded in .github/workflows/windows_arm64.yml.

Complete corresponding source of GNU FriBidi (LGPL):
    fribidi-0.dll is built from the upstream source at tag v1.0.16,
        https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi
    with the vcpkg port of that version, which changes only the build files
    (meson-crosscompile.patch: tables may be pregenerated and are installed;
    no change to the library's own code):
        https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/03e366fb91e38b9432ebd5f8cc79f7c8f55e96ab/ports/fribidi
