libass and the libraries it uses  (Windows packages: draw ASS and SSA subtitles)
================================================================================

tsMuxeR.exe draws ASS and SSA subtitles with libass. libass and the libraries it
needs are shipped as separate dynamic-link libraries placed next to tsMuxeR.exe.
tsMuxeR.exe is not linked against them: it loads ass-9.dll when an ASS or SSA
track is drawn, and works without it (the subtitles are then drawn as plain
text). You may replace any of them with your own build of a compatible version.

How they are built:
    The libraries are built by vcpkg (https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg) from
    packaging/libass/vcpkg.json in this repository, whose builtin-baseline
    fixes every version above, with the build configuration recorded in
    .github/workflows/windows_64.yml (and windows_32.yml).

        https://github.com/fribidi/fribidi
