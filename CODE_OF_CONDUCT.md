# Code of conduct

tsMuxeR is free software, looked after in spare time. The issue tracker works when everyone in it treats the others with respect and keeps to the few rules that make problems possible to find. This page applies to issues, comments, discussions and pull requests of this repository.

## Be respectful

- Write to others as you would like them to write to you. No insults, no swearing at people or at the rules, no threats.
- Disagreement is fine. Say what you think and why, calmly.
- Do not write whole sentences in capital letters. It reads as shouting.
- Not everyone here writes English as their first language. Keep sentences simple, and be patient with those of others.

## Keep to the rules of the tracker

The rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). They are there so that each problem can be found and fixed, not to make reporting harder. In short:

- One issue for one problem. Fill in the form of the issue.
- No whole films, whole discs or links to them. A sample made by "Make a sample..." or `--make-sample` is enough. If more is needed, it is asked for in the issue.
- Post only what you may share. An issue is public.
- Do not post personal data: no e-mail addresses, phone numbers or real names of others, and none of your own that you do not want public.
- Write in the issue on GitHub. Replies by e-mail are posted with the whole quoted e-mail under them, often with names and addresses in it.

The rules are not up for discussion in each issue. If you think a rule should change, open one issue of the kind "Other" and say why.

## Be patient

Replies can take days, more when a test needs special hardware or long runs. A fault is answered when it is understood and, where possible, fixed and tested. Asking again and again does not make it faster.

## What happens when these rules are not kept

The maintainer decides case by case. Depending on what happened:

1. Links to whole files, personal data, and text that breaks this page are removed from the post, or the post is hidden or deleted.
2. A short note in the issue points to this page.
3. Where it goes on, the account may be blocked from this repository, without a further note. The issues it opened stay open where the problem is real: a report is judged by the problem, not by its author.

## Reporting a problem with behaviour

Use "Report content" in the menu of a comment (the three dots) on GitHub. It reaches the maintainer and GitHub.
